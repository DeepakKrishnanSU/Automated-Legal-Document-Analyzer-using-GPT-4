MASTER SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AND CLOUD SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Software Development and Cloud Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of December 1, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

CLIENT:
Apex Financial Group Inc., a financial services corporation with offices at 100 Wall Street, 25th Floor, New York, NY 10005 ("Client").

AND

SERVICE PROVIDER:
Vanguard Cloud Solutions LLC, an enterprise software consultancy with offices at 555 Tech Ridge Boulevard, Suite 300, Austin, TX 78759 ("Service Provider").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES AND PURPOSE
Service Provider shall provide full-lifecycle software engineering, automated pipeline architecture, and cloud infrastructure migration services to modernize Client's real-time risk analytics platform ("Project"). Detailed deliverables and acceptance criteria are specified in attached Statements of Work (SOW).

2. KEY OBLIGATIONS AND DELIVERABLES
(a) Service Provider Obligations:
    - Design, develop, and test microservices architecture compliant with SOC-2 security standards.
    - Guarantee 99.9% uptime for deployed infrastructure during the initial hyper-care stabilization period.
    - Provide complete technical documentation, automated deployment scripts, and developer knowledge transfer sessions.
(b) Client Obligations:
    - Grant Service Provider timely access to required development staging environments, cloud sandboxes, and APIs.
    - Review and provide formal milestone acceptance or specific rejection notices within ten (10) business days of deliverable submission.

3. TERM AND DURATION
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date (December 1, 2026) and shall remain in full force and effect for an initial duration of eighteen (18) months, concluding on May 31, 2028, unless earlier terminated pursuant to Section 6.

4. PAYMENT TERMS AND INVOICING
(a) Contract Value: The total estimated fee for the services defined under SOW-1 is $180,000 (One Hundred Eighty Thousand US Dollars).
(b) Payment Schedule: Client shall pay Service Provider a monthly retainer of $10,000, payable on the first day of each calendar month, with the remaining $60,000 tied to the completion and approval of four milestone deliverables ($15,000 per milestone).
(c) Invoicing: Invoices shall be submitted electronically by Service Provider and are payable Net-30 days from date of receipt. Late balances shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
Both parties agree to treat all business, financial, customer, and proprietary information received from the other party as strictly confidential. Service Provider agrees never to store, transmit, or process Client's unencrypted financial records, and must comply with all applicable data privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

6. TERMINATION CONDITIONS
(a) Termination for Convenience: Client may terminate this Agreement without cause at any time by delivering sixty (60) days advance written notice to Service Provider, subject to paying for all deliverables completed up to the effective termination date.
(b) Termination for Material Breach: Either party may terminate immediately if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within fourteen (14) days of receiving written notice.
(c) Post-Termination Handover: Upon termination, Service Provider shall deliver all current work-in-progress, source code repositories, and documentation to Client within seven (7) business days.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Upon full payment of all undisputed fees, Client shall own all proprietary rights, title, and interest in and to the custom deliverables, source code, and configurations created specifically for Client under this Agreement. Service Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing background tools and libraries.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted under the laws of the State of Delaware, excluding its conflict of laws rules. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement that cannot be resolved amicably within thirty (30) days shall be settled through binding commercial arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the authorized representatives of the parties have executed this Agreement on the Effective Date.
